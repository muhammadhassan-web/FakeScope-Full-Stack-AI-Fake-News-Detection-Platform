Methodology

How FakeScope reads the signal

A classical NLP pipeline — TF-IDF features and supervised classifiers — scored against labeled news text.

1. Clean the text

Input is lowercased, stripped of URLs and noise, tokenized, stopword-filtered, and lightly stemmed so the model sees content words rather than formatting.

2. Vectorize

A TF-IDF vectorizer with unigrams and bigrams (up to 15,000 features) turns cleaned text into a sparse numeric representation.

3. Classify

Logistic Regression, Multinomial Naive Bayes, and Random Forest are trained; the best F1 model is served for live predictions with class probabilities.

4. Interpret carefully

Scores reflect linguistic similarity to training patterns — not ground truth. Low confidence means the text is ambiguous; always verify with trusted sources.
